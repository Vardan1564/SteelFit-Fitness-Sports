Our Story

WE ARE
STEELFIT

Born from the iron floors of Mumbai's underground fitness scene. Built for athletes who know that greatness isn't given — it's forged. Since 2018, we've been putting world-class equipment in the hands of everyday champions.

🏋️
How It Started

FOUNDED IN
A GARAGE GYM

It started with a frustration. Our founder, Arjun Verma — a competitive powerlifter — couldn't find quality equipment without paying luxury prices imported from abroad. He spent 2 years sourcing, testing, and rejecting hundreds of suppliers before finding partners who matched his obsessive standards.

SteelFit launched in 2018 from a 200 sq.ft. garage in Andheri, Mumbai with just 12 products. Today we ship to every corner of India, serve 12,000+ members, and stock over 250 premium fitness products — all maintaining the same uncompromising quality Arjun demanded from day one.

Every product in our catalog has been personally tested by our team of certified trainers, physiotherapists, and competitive athletes. If it doesn't survive our internal abuse tests, it never reaches your door.

Our Mission

To democratize access to premium fitness equipment across India — making world-class gear affordable, accessible, and available to every serious athlete regardless of their geography or budget. We believe excellence in training shouldn't be a privilege.

🔭

Our Vision

To build India's most trusted fitness brand — one that athletes turn to not just for equipment, but for guidance, community, and inspiration. By 2028, we aim to have 1 million SteelFit members actively training and transforming their lives across the country.
